The Mission
NovaBleu Kids exists to build creative confidence in children — through structured art sessions that prioritize:
Expressionover technique
Collaborationover competition
Identityover imitation

Kids are already creative. They don't need to be fixed — they need an environment that teaches them to see, personalize, and own their creative decisions.
