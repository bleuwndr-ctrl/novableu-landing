Why I Created NovaBleu Kids

I'm Charles, founder of NovaBleu Kids, and this program is the class I wish I had when I was twelve.

Growing up in Los Angeles, I loved to draw. Art was everywhere, murals, art walks, festivals my parents took me to. It shaped how I moved through the world before I even understood it.

When I was twelve, my parents enrolled me in an art program. I was so excited. But between the cost and the drive, we could only make it a couple times before we had to let it go. I still remember how disappointing that was, wanting something that badly and watching it slip away for reasons that had nothing to do with talent or desire.

Looking back, what I actually needed wasn't more art classes. It was someone who could help me take what was already in my head and get it onto paper, in my own way. I never really found that, not in school, not in college.

I stopped drawing for almost ten years while I grew up, worked in healthcare, became an adult. But I never stopped noticing kids doodling, that same spark I used to carry myself. And I noticed something else too: today's kids have more access to inspiration and technology than I ever dreamed of, but it's harder than ever for them to find their own voice underneath all that noise.

That's the gap I built NovaBleu Kids to close. Not to pull kids away from technology, but to hand it back to them the right way around, as a tool that sharpens their imagination instead of feeding them everyone else's. I want to be the guide I never had.

Everything I've collected along the way, drawing since I was a kid, my years in behavior health, working in tech, it all led here.

NovaBleu Kids is the program I wish existed when I was twelve years old.
