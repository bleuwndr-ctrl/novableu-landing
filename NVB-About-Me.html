"I'm not a veteran educator with a proven system and 500 alumni. I'm a first-time founder who spent 20 years building something I didn't have a name for — and finally figured out what it was."

Growing up, I didn't learn art to feel something. I learned art to prove something. Could you make it look real? Could you copy it correctly? Art was a performance, not a language.

It took years of self-teaching, writing, and building my own creative identity to understand what art actually is: not a showcase of skill — a way of processing the world and putting your specific version of it on paper. That transformation changed everything about how I create, and it made me ask one question: what if kids learned that from the beginning?

Working with kids daily as a behavior technician, I noticed their drawings already have personality, energy, and a distinct voice. They aren't waiting to be taught how to be creative — they're waiting for an environment where that creativity has somewhere to go.

NovaBleu Kids is that question turned into a program. I teach every session. I iterate after every cohort. There is no layer between the product and the person who built it.
