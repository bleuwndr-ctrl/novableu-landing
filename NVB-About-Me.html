Why I Created NovaBleu Kids

Growing up in Los Angeles, I loved to draw. The city was full of art everywhere I looked, murals, communities, art walks, and it became part of how I moved through the world. My parents used to take me to cultural festivals, film screenings, and art shows, and all of that shaped who I was becoming before I even understood it.

I remember my parents trying so hard to nurture that in me. When I was about twelve, they enrolled me in an art program, and I was so excited. But between the cost and the drive, we could only make it a couple times before we had to let it go. I still remember how disappointing that was. Wanting something that badly and watching it slip away for reasons that had nothing to do with talent or desire.

Looking back, what I actually needed wasn't more art classes. It was someone who could help me take what was already in my head and actually get it onto paper. Someone to help me tell my story, in my own way. I never really found that. Not in school, not in the classes I took growing up, not even in college when I studied graphic design. I could learn technique all day long. Nobody ever taught me how to see my own world and build it.

I stopped drawing for almost ten years while I grew up, worked in healthcare, became an adult. But I never stopped noticing kids doodling, in classrooms, in hallways, that same spark I used to carry around myself.

And I started noticing something else too: today's kids have more access to inspiration and technology than I ever dreamed of, but somehow it's harder than ever for them to find their own voice underneath all that noise.

That's the gap I built NovaBleu Kids to close. Not to pull kids away from technology, but to hand it back to them the right way around, as a tool that sharpens their own imagination, instead of one more thing feeding them everyone else's. I want to be the guide I never had. Someone who doesn't teach a kid how to draw, but helps them see the world they're already carrying around inside them, and shows them how to build it.

Everything I've collected along the way, art since I was a kid, a decade of drawing, tutoring here and there, my years in behavior health, working in tech, it all led here. NovaBleu Kids is the program I wish existed when I was twelve years old.
